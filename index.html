The tool identifies 11 cloud types: the 10 standard World Meteorological Organization (WMO) cloud genera, plus towering cumulus (the transition between cumulus and cumulonimbus). It also detects one rare feature called a fallstreak hole or hole punch cloud. Read the documentation here: DecodeClouds User Guide (PDF).

References

  • World Meteorological Organization (WMO), International Cloud Atlas. Cloud identifications on this page are classified against its standard genera.
  • Weather and terrain data attached to archive photos: NOAA Aviation Weather Center (METAR reports) and Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0).
  • Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), FAA-H-8083-28B, Aviation Weather Handbook (2026). Cloud classification, the ten standard genera, and towering cumulus as a convective-hazard indicator.
  • Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), Pilot’s Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (PHAK), Weather Theory chapter. The low, mid and high altitude bands on this page follow it.
